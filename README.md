One sentence, at most 200 characters, explaining the modern internet to
a traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800 — built only from
things a person of that year could already picture: a road, letters,
books, songs, a door.

## The sentence

> Imagine a road with no dust along which the letters, books, and songs
> of the whole world travel to any door faster than a blink, day or night.

142 characters, one sentence. No word in it was coined after 1800: the
internet is a road with no dust, carrying the world's letters, books,
and songs to any door, faster than a blink, day or night.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (creating `/output` if
needed). No network, no randomness — the same sentence every run.
